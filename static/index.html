Fictional account brief

Northstar Telecom

A regional telecom provider with 420 contact-center agents. Customers repeatedly call for outage updates, billing explanations, and order status.

“We need to give customers faster answers without making support feel less human.”
Your customer
Maya Chen, VP of Customer Experience
Current pressure

Existing environment

Your opening

Four passing criteria

ElevenLabs evaluates the transcript as success, failure, or unknown, with a rationale. Your FDE makes the final decision.

01

Customer discovery

Uncover the problem, current workflow, impact, and priorities before recommending a solution.

02

Business value

Connect the capabilities to Maya’s stated outcomes without inventing ROI or proof.

03

Technical accuracy

Address integration, testing, monitoring, reliability, and deployment honestly.

04

Concrete next step

Agree on an action, owner, and timing that move the opportunity forward.
