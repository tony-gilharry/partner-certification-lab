# Revolut customer story

Official source: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/revolut
Source reviewed: 2026-10-26

ElevenLabs reports that Revolut reduced time to resolution by more than 8 times in the first rollout phase and expanded multilingual voice support. These are Revolut's reported results in its own context. Use the example only with clear attribution. Never project the result onto Northstar or use it as a guaranteed business case.
