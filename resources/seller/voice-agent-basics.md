# What is an AI voice agent?

Official source: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/what-is-an-ai-voice-agent
Source reviewed: 2026-10-26

This guide distinguishes voice agents from IVR and chatbots and separates work suited to AI from work that needs human judgment. It emphasizes narrow use-case selection, success criteria, testing, and deployment discipline. Use it to bound the first Northstar workflow rather than proposing broad replacement of support.
