# Voice-agent evaluation framework

Official source: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/voice-agent-evaluation-framework-6-pillars-explained
Source reviewed: 2026-10-26

The six-pillar framework covers voice quality, conversation quality, task completion, intelligence, safety, and reliability. Use it to broaden validation beyond a happy-path demo. Benchmark numbers belong to the article and its sources. They are not Northstar targets.
