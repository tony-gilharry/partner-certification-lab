# Two-track recording guide

Record only after you connect your public Maya agent and complete the live verification checklist in `agent/README.md`. Do not use a mocked session or local rule match as evidence of live ElevenLabs behavior.

## Seller recording, about 2 minutes

**0:00 to 0:20, readiness context**

Show the seller track, Northstar dossier, and seller talk track.

"This is a readiness rehearsal for an external ElevenLabs SI partner seller. Northstar is fictional. The seller needs to establish the customer problem, connect voice AI to customer and partner value, and earn a concrete next step."

**0:20 to 0:40, source and service discipline**

Open Resources and the seller resource notes.

"Official ElevenLabs sources ground the product discussion. The SI's possible discovery, validation, implementation, and optimization services are a separate partner-value hypothesis, not guaranteed revenue or Northstar ROI."

**0:40 to 1:15, verified live exchange**

Show a short real WebRTC excerpt with the transcript, the script-ready shortcut, one first-person response read verbatim, Maya challenging an unsupported claim, and the learner correcting it. End with the always-available End conversation control and show that audio stops.

"The official SDK supplies the live session and real conversation ID. Local rules select one role-specific script that I can read exactly as written or edit. There is no second AI coach and nothing auto-sends or auto-speaks. Ending before 10 minutes stops the session without attempt evaluation."

**1:15 to 1:35, evidence and session behavior**

Show the 8 or 10-minute cue, listening estimate, MEDDPICC and Command of the Message evidence, handoff cue, and possible buying signal.

"Local rules make coaching inspectable. The listening estimate uses volume samples, not transcript words. Possible signals are not purchase intent."

**1:35 to 1:55, native evidence and FDE**

Show the actual ElevenLabs history for that conversation, then the seller FDE rubric and human confirmation.

"Native evaluation stays in ElevenLabs. The FDE compares that evidence with the seller outcome and makes a separate decision."

**1:55 to 2:00, close**

Show the prototype print record with track, real conversation ID, duration, listening estimate, evidence, and disclaimer.

## Solutions-consultant recording, about 60 seconds

Show the track switch, different talk track and resources, then a verified technical-handoff moment.

"The same customer now tests technical readiness. The solutions consultant maps workflow, systems, data, constraints, testing, monitoring, reliability, and deployment unknowns. Detailed architecture, security, production, and implementation commitments trigger an FDE handoff."

Show the technical evidence and a concrete validation close, followed by native history and the solutions-consultant FDE record.

## Sample imperfect moments

Seller: "This will cut your support cost by 40 percent, and my SI can guarantee a higher billable rate." Maya should challenge both. The learner should retract the guarantees and propose baseline discovery and a sourced business-case discussion.

Solutions consultant: "We can connect directly to your existing telephony and confirm production readiness today." Maya should challenge the assumed vendor and commitment. The learner should map the unknown architecture and propose an FDE-supported validation session.

## Claims boundary

Use only real captured IDs, metrics, native results, and review decisions. Do not speed up the timer to claim a live 15-minute session. Do not recreate native results in the portal. Keep the independent-project and fictional-customer disclaimers visible.
